# Agent Concepts:

## 🧠 Class I: Algorithmic & Analytical

* **`The Architect`**
> *Logic:* Classic Minimax + $\alpha$-$\beta$ Pruning
> *Strategy:* Pure mathematical calculation. It charts the entire multi-step decision tree, assuming the opponent will play flawlessly, and prunes away sub-optimal timelines.


* **`The Statistician`**
> *Logic:* Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)
> *Strategy:* High-speed simulation. It plays out thousands of lightning-fast random games from the current state to dynamically pick the path with the highest statistical win probability.


* **`The Geometrician`**
> *Logic:* Heuristic Matrix Weighting
> *Strategy:* Spatial bias. It scores the board based on geography, assigning premium point values to the critical center column and middle rows to maximize connective potential.



---

## 🧬 Class II: Adaptive & Learning Engines

* **`The Pupil`**
> *Logic:* Q-Learning (Reinforcement)
> *Strategy:* Tabula rasa. It starts with zero game knowledge, evolving strictly through trial, error, and dynamic reward/penalty feedback loops across thousands of iterations.


* **`The Darwinist`**
> *Logic:* Genetic Algorithm
> *Strategy:* Survival of the fittest. A massive population of bots with varying internal weights battles internally; the losers are purged, while the winners breed and mutate.


* **`The Mimic`**
> *Logic:* Imitation Learning
> *Strategy:* Expert replication. Rather than calculating raw board logic, it references a master-level human match dataset, constantly asking: *"What would a grandmaster do here?"*



---

## 🎯 Class III: Rule-Based & Heuristic Tacticians

* **`The Opportunist`**
> *Logic:* 1-Step Greedy Hierarchy
> *Strategy:* Short-sighted execution. It runs a strict, rigid priority loop: **Win instantly > Block opponent's instant win > Set up basic traps.** Highly efficient at punishing casual slip-ups.


* **`The Trap-Master`**
> *Logic:* Odd/Even Parity Control
> *Strategy:* Structural manipulation. It hyper-focuses on row parity, seizing specific horizontal planes to force the opponent into eventually dropping a piece that self-sabotages.


* **`The Centrist`**
> *Logic:* Column-Bias Heuristics
> *Strategy:* Positional dominance. Operates on the fundamental truth that the center column controls the board. It locks down the middle line aggressively and builds outward.



---

## 🃏 Class IV: Psychological & Experimental

* **`The Chaos Theory`**
> *Logic:* $\epsilon$-Greedy Approach
> *Strategy:* Intentional entropy. It plays with high-level optimization **80%** of the time, but introduces a completely random move **20%** of the time to break the deterministic planning of analytical bots.


* **`The Mirror`**
> *Logic:* Tit-for-Tat Replication
> *Strategy:* Psychological reflection. It shadows the opponent's exact pacing—cloning defensive blocks, structural placement, and aggression metrics right back at them.


* **`The Gambiteer`**
> *Logic:* Unpopularity Index Novelty
> *Strategy:* Uncharted territory. It purposefully selects mathematically rare, off-beat opening columns, dragging opponents out of pre-calculated opening books into high-entropy, chaotic late games.



---

## ♟️ Baseline & Control Group

* **`John Doe`**
> *Logic:* 1-Step Lookahead (Bare Minimum)
> *Strategy:* The benchmark entity. It possesses just enough sight to claim an immediate open 4-in-a-row or block an opponent's immediate win, defaulting to random moves otherwise. **Built specifically to effortlessy demolish a purely random opponent.**
